## How to record (10 minutes)

1. **Teleprompter trick:** play `video/AetherRidge_demo_30s.mp4` on your computer (sound off) and read each subtitle the moment it appears, while recording on your phone. This keeps you in sync automatically.
2. **Room:** a small, soft room (a wardrobe full of clothes, or under a blanket) kills echo. Turn off fans and air conditioning.
3. **Phone:** Voice Memos (iPhone) or Recorder (Android), phone 15–20 cm from your mouth, slightly to the side so your breath does not hit it.
4. **Delivery:** smile while speaking (it is audible), stress the numbers ("**eighty-eight** percent", "**zero**"), pause half a second after "zero". Calm and confident beats fast.
5. **Takes:** record 3 full takes; send me the best one (any format: m4a, mp3, wav). If one line is bad, re-record just that line and send it too.
6. **Length:** if you run over 30 s, speak a little faster rather than cutting words; I can also trim silences.

Chinese narration is allowed (subtitles are already in English), but English narration is easier for the international judges.

## Why the video is built this way (judging logic)

* **First 3 seconds:** name + team (the submission guide suggests stating them) over the most striking visual (real 3D terrain), so a judge knows what this is before they can skip.
* **One problem sentence, then numbers:** judges remember concrete figures — 88 % vs 0 %, 53 % → 78 %, 14 days — more than feature lists.
* **Only the real product on screen:** no slides, no stock footage, no music to license; every shot is the live app with real NASA data.
* **Credibility at the end:** "checked against NASA's own maps" plus the data sources on the end card speak to *Validity* and *Relevance*.
* **Call to action:** the live URL stays on screen for the last 3 seconds.
* **Hard limits respected:** 30.0 s, English subtitles burned in, 1080p, no copyrighted material.
